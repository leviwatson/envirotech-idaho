actions:
  heading: Do these first
  intro: "In order. If you can only do one, do the first."
  items:
    - title: Shut the water off at the main
      detail: "If water is still arriving, stop it. The main shutoff is usually where the supply enters the house — a crawlspace, a garage wall, a utility room, or a box near the street. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Everything else waits until this is done."
    - title: Kill power to the affected rooms
      detail: "Switch off the breakers for the wet areas at the panel. Do not wade through standing water to reach a panel, and do not touch a breaker while standing in water. If the panel is in the wet area, leave it and tell us when you call."
    - title: Get people and pets out of the water
      detail: "Out of the wet rooms entirely, not just off the floor. Wet drywall and saturated ceilings can come down without much warning, and a ceiling holding water above a room is a reason to stay out of that room."
    - title: Lift what you can, photograph what you cannot
      detail: "Get furniture legs onto blocks or foil, lift rugs, and move anything irreplaceable up and out. Photograph the rooms before you move much — insurers work from what the damage looked like before anyone started tidying."
    - title: Do not use a household vacuum
      detail: "A shop vac rated for water is fine. A household vacuum is not, and running one in standing water is a genuine electrical risk. Fans are also worth leaving off until power in the area is sorted."
  caution: "If water is anywhere near the electrical panel, if the ceiling is bulging, or if you are unsure about any of this, stop and call. Nothing on this list is worth a shock or a collapse, and talking it through takes a minute."
